Guilt-free bookscrolling.

anquar turns your EPUBs into a vertical feed: one screen of a book per swipe, on your phone, offline.

Open anquar See it read

A book, one screen at a time.

This is the opening of Moby-Dick, cut into cards by the same engine the app uses. Swipe the page, tap it for the rail, and slide the lamp to dim it.

Cut to fit your screen

Whole paragraphs where they fit. A long one breaks at a sentence, and a heading stays with what it opens.

It counts once you've read it

A card becomes an anquar after it has been on screen long enough to read at 600 words a minute. Flicking past doesn't count.

Your place, to the word

Change the type size or the margins and the book is laid out again, with you back on the words you were reading.

Opens where you left off

Long books open at your chapter, not at page one. The next chapters load as you get near them.

Close the core, every day.

The mark is your daily meter. Each line is a quarter of your goal, filled from the core outward, so a full mark means today is done.

Ask about a word

Select a passage and tap the words you mean. The answer comes in over the page: a few words of meaning first, then the detail.

and especially whenever my hypos get such an upper hand of me, that it requires a strong moral principle to prevent me from deliberately stepping into the street
hypos

Low spirits; a fit of gloom.

Short for hypochondria, which in Melville's day meant melancholy. Ishmael means the moods that send him to sea.

Bring your books

Share an EPUB to anquar from any Android app, or pick one from your files. DRM-free books only.

Your books never leave your phone.

No account, no upload

Books, places, saves and streaks live in your browser's storage on this phone. There is nothing to sign up for, and no cookies.

Reads offline

Once it's on your home screen, anquar opens and reads with no connection at all. A flight is a fine place for a chapter.

Only what you ask it to send

Explain sends your words to Gemini with your own key, only when you ask. The daily reminder, if you turn it on, keeps the time you chose, your time zone, your phone's push address and whether today's goal is closed on a small server. Never a book. Cloudflare counts page visits, with no cookies.

Put it where your thumb already goes.

anquar is a web app you add to your home screen. It opens full screen, like any other app, and keeps working offline.

Open anquar
Android, in Chrome
  1. Open anquar and tap Install app, or Add to Home screen from the menu.
  2. anquar now shows up in the share sheet. Send it an EPUB from Files, Telegram or your browser.
iPhone, in Safari
  1. Open anquar and tap Share, then Add to Home Screen.
  2. Open it from the home screen and add books with the + button.
